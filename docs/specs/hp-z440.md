# HP Z440

- **Form factor:** a single tower. **The facts below are for that.**
- **CPU:**
  - Chipset Intel C612. "Single LGA2011 R3" ([QS]).
  - Xeon E5-1600 v3/v4 and E5-2600 v3. The processor table also lists the E5-2637 v4 and E5-2623 v4, so the doc is inconsistent on "E5-2600 v4" ([QS]).
  - Max listed TDP 140 W ([QS]).
  - "the HP Z440 Workstation does not support dual processor configurations" ([QS]).
  - BIOS for v4: not stated in the pages read. The change log (Nov 2016) says "Removed Intel Xeon v3 processors" from the offered configurations ([QS]).
- **Memory:**
  - "ONLY registered DDR4 DIMMs are supported". "DDR3 DIMMs ARE NOT SUPPORTED". ECC registered (RDIMM) ([QS]). UDIMM and LRDIMM are not mentioned.
  - 8 slots, "Maximum Memory Supports up to 128GB", max 16 GB per DIMM in this version ([QS]).
  - Speeds 1600/1866/2133/2400 MT/s, set by the CPU. 2400 appears only with v4 CPUs ([QS]).
  - More than 4 DIMMs requires the Memory Cooling Solution (J2R51AV / J2R52AA) ([QS]).
- **Built-in graphics:** "Integrated Graphics No" ([QS]). A discrete card is needed for display (inference, not stated).
- **Expansion slots** ([QS]):
  - Slot 1: PCIe Gen2 x1, open-ended, full-height, half-length.
  - Slot 2: PCIe Gen3 x16, full-height, full-length with extender.
  - Slot 3: PCIe Gen2 x4, open-ended.
  - Slot 4: PCIe Gen3 x8, open-ended.
  - Slot 5: PCIe Gen3 x16.
  - Slot 6: PCI 32-bit/33 MHz.
  - Slots 2–6 are full-height, full-length with the extender.
  - Graphics power: the 525 W PSU has no 6-pin graphics cable. The 700 W PSU has two graphics power cables and supports up to 225 W of graphics ([QS]).
  - "A 700W PSU is required for any K40 configuration". For the Quadro P5000 "the 700W power supply option must be used" ([QS]).
  - Several cards (M4000, M5000, K4200, dual W4300) require the "HP Z440 Fan and Front Card Guide Kit" ([QS]).
- **Storage:**
  - No native M.2. M.2 is only via PCIe cards (Z Turbo Drive G2, Quad Pro) ([QS]).
  - Bays: 2 internal 3.5", 2 external 5.25" and 1 dedicated 9.5 mm slim ODD bay. 3rd/4th drives use the external bays ([QS]).
  - SATA: 6 ports at 6 Gb/s. The drive-interface section also has a separate line, "4 sSATA @6Gb/s, Supports RAID 0,1,10 and NCQ." ([QS]). The source does not say whether that line and the six-port controller describe the same ports, so reading them as conflicting is the researcher's interpretation (inference, not stated).
  - SAS needs an optional PCIe controller ([QS]).
  - The 525 W PSU has power for only 2 HDDs by default. 3rd/4th drives need a 4-pin-to-dual-SATA cable ([QS]).
- **Power supply:** 525 W (85 %) or 700 W (90 %), wide-ranging, active PFC ([QS]). Proprietary connector: not stated.
- **Networking:** "Integrated Intel I-218 Gbit LAN" (I218LM). Wi-Fi is only via PCIe cards (Intel 7260, Intel 8260) ([QS]).
- **Upgrade trip-ups the docs state:**
  - RDIMM only; no DDR3.
  - Memory cooling for more than 4 DIMMs.
  - 525 W PSU has no GPU power cable.
  - 700 W PSU tops out at 225 W of graphics.
  - Fan/card-guide kit needed for many GPUs.
  - DDR4-2400 only with v4 CPUs, and not every v4: the E5-1603 v4, E5-1607 v4 and E5-2623 v4 rows say 2133 ([QS]).
  - No M.2 on the board.
