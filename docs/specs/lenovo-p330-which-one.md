# Lenovo ThinkStation P330 (which one?)

"P330" covers five separate PSREF entries: **P330 Tower**, **P330 SFF**, **P330 Tiny** (first generation), and **P330 Tower Gen 2** and **P330 SFF Gen 2** (adding 9th-gen Core and Xeon E-2200) [S0]. What each first-generation PSREF lists (corrected on a second check, 2026-11-20): the **P330 Tower** and **P330 SFF** say "Or up to one 8th Intel Core family processor" (i3, i5, i7; no 9th gen), alongside Xeon E (E-2100 on the SFF sheet) and a Pentium Gold G5400 via special bid [S1][S2]; the **P330 Tiny** says "Up to one Intel Core i9, i7, i5, or i3 processor" (8th- and 9th-gen in its CPU list) and lists no Xeon [S3]. I saw no "P330 Tiny Gen 2" in the search results, but I haven't confirmed that none exists. Each entry has its own file: `lenovo-p330-tower.md`, `lenovo-p330-sff.md`, `lenovo-p330-tiny.md`, `lenovo-p330-tower-gen-2.md` and `lenovo-p330-sff-gen-2.md`. Check the label to tell Gen 1 from Gen 2 (writer's advice, not from a source). The CPU fitted only identifies Gen 2 if it is a 9th-gen Core or a Xeon E-22xx, because both Gen 2 sheets also list 8th-gen Core and some E-2100 Xeons such as the E-2174G (inference from the CPU lists, not stated by Lenovo).
