    Fit him to the wall

    1. 1Drag each numbered corner onto a corner of the wall space.
    2. 2Pick a corner and nudge it with the arrow keys, a pixel at a time, or ten with Shift.
    3. 3Save. He stays fitted on this screen until the projector moves.

    With the TV remote, OK picks each corner in turn, then the Save button, and OK again saves. Hold an arrow to move a corner faster.

    Everything outside the shape stays black, so the wall only shows Jarvis.
